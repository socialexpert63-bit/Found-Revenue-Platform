Framework

Seven parts. Every finding tested.

Decision, Context, Evidence, Analysis, Challenge, Verification, Output. The analyst argues against its own findings, and a named human verifies before anything is reported.

⚖️

Challenge stage

Alternative explanations, assumptions and missing data are surfaced.

✅

Human verification

Metrics, sources and calculations are signed off by a reviewer.

📄

Evidence-led output

Executive summary, actions, estimated value and confidence notes.
